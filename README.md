# Shawnstuck Restored
Shawnstuck Restored intends to restore the original Shawnstuck game to a playable and completable state.

Originally released on 06/07/24, Shawnstuck was the result of two developers (plus some additional help) attempting to make a game about a friend. The game development time ran for a total of 4 weeks, with final adjustments being made minutes before (and in some cases during) presentation.

It containted a myriad of bugs, technical and balancing issues, an incomplete and rushed ending sequence, and low quality music present during the showcase.

Over two years later, with refined and matured tastes, the two same developers have come back to restore the functionality of the original game, making minor combat, reduce poor pacing, and remove unwanted or low quality content.

# Installation

Simply navigate towards the [latest release](https://github.com/Naughty-Knight/Shawnstuck-Restored/releases/latest) and download to game.

Alternatively, if you own a copy of RPG Maker MV, you can `git clone` the repository and build the game yourself, analyze the source, make changes, or simply pry and look for secrets.
